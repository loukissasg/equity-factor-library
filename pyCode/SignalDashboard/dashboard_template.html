5. How to read this

Long-short return

Return of the top portfolio minus the bottom portfolio, signed so it is long the side the original paper says earns more. It costs nothing to set up (the short pays for the long), so it is already an excess return.

t-stat

Mean divided by its standard error: how many standard errors the average is away from zero. Above about 2 is unlikely to be luck at the 5% level; with hundreds of signals tested, a bar of 3 is safer.

Sharpe ratio

Average return per unit of volatility, annualised (monthly mean / monthly std × √12). The US market has historically been around 0.4-0.5, which is a useful yardstick.

Alpha

The average return left over after a regression on known factors (market, size, value, ...). A significant alpha means the signal is not just a repackaging of those factors.

Drawdown

How far the $1 investment has fallen below its previous high. The maximum drawdown is the worst such fall: the pain you would have had to sit through.
